Connect an app

Bring a passage into your practice.

Connect an AI assistant to your Incord account using MCP (Model Context Protocol). Once you sign in and approve access, it can read your library and add passages for you. You can keep practicing in Incord as usual.

Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Enter Incord as the name and https://incord.jesklabs.com/mcp as the URL.
  3. Connect, sign in to Incord, and review the permissions before approving.

Custom connector availability and menu names can vary by plan, workspace policy and app version.

Claude Code

Add Incord as an HTTP MCP server from your terminal:

claude mcp add --transport http incord https://incord.jesklabs.com/mcp

Then run /mcp in Claude Code and follow the authentication prompts to sign in and approve access.

ChatGPT and other clients

If your client supports remote MCP over Streamable HTTP with OAuth, use the same server URL: https://incord.jesklabs.com/mcp. Follow its connection and authorization steps. ChatGPT support may depend on its developer mode, your plan and workspace settings; not every client supports this connection.

Try a prompt

  • “Add Romans 8:28–39 to Incord as one passage.”
  • “Add the Beatitudes, Matthew 5:3–12, to Incord verse by verse.”
  • “How is my memorization group doing this week?”
  • “Tell the Incord team I’d like a dark-mode schedule.”

You can also ask what is already in your library. Review your assistant's proposed passages and translation when adding something new.

What you allow

The authorization screen offers read and write permissions. Read access lets the connected app see your library, practice information and your groups. Group results respect every member’s title-sharing preference and exclude item text, detailed reviews, email addresses and account IDs. The connector cannot create, join or manage groups. Write access lets it add memory items. It cannot edit or delete your existing items. Any valid connected app can also send feedback you ask it to share, including with read-only library access. Feedback sends your message, its kind, the connected app’s name, and an optional reply address you explicitly provide—not your library contents.

ESV text stays on your device. The connector does not return or store ESV text; open Incord on your device to read and practice ESV passages.

Disconnect whenever you like

In Incord, open Settings → Connected apps and revoke the app's access. Revocation stops future access; it cannot remove information an assistant has already received. You can also remove the connector from your assistant's settings.
